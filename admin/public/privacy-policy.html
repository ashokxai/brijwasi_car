1. Scope

This Policy applies to:

  • The Brijwasi Car Bazaar Android application available on Google Play;
  • Account registration, login, browsing, favourites/wishlist, selling/listing cars, and related in-app features;

This Policy does not control third-party websites, apps, or services that we do not operate (for example WhatsApp or your mobile carrier), even if the App opens them at your request.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only information needed to operate a used-car marketplace and to keep accounts secure.

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account & identity: mobile phone number, email address, and name/display name.
  • Authentication: one-time passwords (OTP) sent to your phone for verification; email/password credentials where used for secondary login.
  • Vehicle listings (Sell Car): brand/model, year, kilometres driven, fuel type, transmission, ownership, city, insurance details (if provided), price, and other specification fields you enter.
  • Photos: images of vehicles you upload from camera or gallery (typically 3–10 photos per listing).
  • Support messages: information you send when contacting us by phone, WhatsApp, or email.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device & technical data: device model, operating system version, app version, IP address, language/locale, crash/diagnostic information, and basic usage events needed for security and reliability.
  • Auth tokens: session/authentication tokens stored securely on your device to keep you signed in.
  • Firebase / Google services identifiers: identifiers used by Firebase Authentication (and related Google services) to verify your phone number and secure the App.

2.3 Information we do not intentionally collect

  • Precise GPS location tracking for advertising;
  • Contacts, SMS content, call logs, or microphone audio (beyond optional camera use for photos);
  • Payment card numbers inside the App (we do not process in-app card payments for car purchases);
  • Sensitive categories such as health, biometric templates, or political opinions.

4. App Permissions

Depending on your Android version and how you use features, the App may request:

  • Internet: required to load listings and authenticate.
  • Camera: optional; used only when you choose to photograph a car for a listing.
  • Photos / media access: optional; used only when you choose to pick images from your gallery for a listing. On newer Android versions, the system photo picker may be used so we do not need broad storage access.

You can deny optional permissions. Core browsing may still work, but Sell Car photo upload may be limited.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only as follows:

  • Service providers / processors who help us run the App under contractual confidentiality obligations, including:
    • Google Firebase / Google Cloud — phone OTP authentication, app integrity/security checks, and related infrastructure;
    • Cloud hosting & database providers — hosting our API and storing account/listing data;
    • Analytics / crash tooling (if enabled via Firebase or similar) — aggregated or technical diagnostics.
  • Business communications you initiate: if you tap Call or WhatsApp, your device opens those apps and any information you send is governed by those providers’ policies.
  • Sharing features: if you use Share on a listing, content you choose to share is sent through your selected sharing destination.
  • Legal & safety: if required by law, regulation, court order, or to protect rights, safety, and integrity of users or our business.
  • Business transfer: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, subject to continued protection of personal data.

Listing photos and non-sensitive vehicle details you submit may be shown publicly in the App to other users as part of the marketplace.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained while your account remains active.
  • Vehicle listings and images are retained while the listing is active and for a reasonable period afterward for dispute handling, audit, and legal compliance.
  • Security logs and technical records may be retained for a limited period for fraud prevention and troubleshooting.
  • When you request deletion (see Section 8), we delete or anonymise personal data except where retention is required by law or legitimate unresolved disputes.

7. Data Security

We use commercially reasonable safeguards, including encrypted transport (HTTPS) for API traffic, hashed passwords where passwords are stored, restricted staff access, and secure token storage on device. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; you use the App at your own risk subject to applicable law.
